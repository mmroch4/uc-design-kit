[XMAS] Christmas Tree

The top-3 students will get a prize: a backpack with 3 pins from the 2019 ICPC World Finals and also a chance to have a snack with Prof. Pedro Ribeiro. For the purposes of receiving this prize you should submit until 23:59 of 2026 January 31st
(the problem will stil be available afterwards, but without counting towards the prizes)

If the best student gets a better score than Prof. Pedro Ribeiro solution (currently 184 bytes, but the professor might still work to improve it) will addicionally receive a special prize: a lunch with the professor, where he will pay the student a "francesinha".
