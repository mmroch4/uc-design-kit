Information for the 1st practical test

General information

Method
Individual. Practical problems for which you submit Python code to Mooshak and receive feedback that might include public test cases, like in the practical classes.
Language
All problem statements are available in English and in Portuguese.
Environment
Linux, with Python available at the command line and the usual editors and IDEs. You have access to documentation (a local copy of the Python docs) and to a local copy of the lecture notebooks (a static HTML version).
Scoring
Worth 4 points out of 20 (20% of your grade). See the calculation formula of the final grade.

You will have 7 small exercises in Mooshak worth 20% + 20% + 15% + 15% + 15% + 10% + 5%. In each exercise there might be partial scoring (e.g. passes some tests, fails on others).

When submitting, you will know the score you got on all tests. Your grade on a problem is the grade of your best submission on that problem (the one with the highest score), and you can submit as many times as you want (with 60 seconds between submissions).
